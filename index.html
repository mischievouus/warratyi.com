About the Project

This project is a personal soundboard built from poetry, observation, and lived thought. Each release functions less like a single song and more like a captured moment of reflection—where ideas about society, identity, attention, and behaviour are translated into sound. It is not driven by trend or genre convention, but by themes and questions, using music as a way to document inner dialogue in a public space. The body of work sits under Warratyi at Warratyi Black Label as an evolving archive of emotional and conceptual sketches.

The way the music is made comes from writing first, not production. Ideas are formed as text, spoken rhythm, or poetic structure, then shaped into musical form through atmosphere, pacing, and tone. Production follows the meaning rather than leading it—piano, voice, distortion, and space are chosen to match the emotional weight of the words. Each track is built like a scene or statement, layered gradually until the feeling of the idea is fully translated into sound.
